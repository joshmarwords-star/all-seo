01

The goal

You said it best: the real game-changer isn't ranking Sylvie for “engagement rings.” It's becoming indispensable to your retailers — the way Gabriel & Co. did on their way to 1,500+ stores. If Sylvie can help a jeweler bring customers through their door, that jeweler never leaves, and the ones down the road want in too.

“Grow Sylvie from ~400 retailers to 800 — by being the supplier that also brings shoppers into each retailer's store.”

Everything below serves that one outcome.

02

The opportunity we see

Two things stood out when we looked under the hood.

  • You already have real authority — it's just aimed at the wrong targets. Sylvie sits at a Domain Rating of 50 (Ahrefs) / Authority Score 41 (SEMrush). That's genuinely strong. But much of it is spent on informational traffic (“stones for July born” — 40,000 searches a month that don't sell a ring) and on head terms the pure-B2C players own and a B2B brand structurally can't win. We don't need to build authority from scratch — we need to redeploy the authority you already have toward terms that map to your business.
  • Your retailer landing pages are a hidden asset. You already have a page per retailer location. Some rank on page one today (Des Moines). They're simply under-optimised — so the fastest wins require improving what exists, not building anything new.
03

The plan — two engines

Engine 1 — Retailer enablement · make Sylvie indispensable

Help each retailer sell locally: optimise their Sylvie landing page to rank for the local “engagement rings [city]” searches, then extend to their own Google Business Profile and local SEO, then drive walk-ins. In your words — we become their agency as well as their supplier.

Engine 2 — Retailer acquisition · get from 400 to 800

Reach jeweler owners directly and sign new accounts — using your target-jeweler list to build custom audiences on Meta and LinkedIn — plus consumer demand that makes jewelers want to carry the line. The jewelry world may be a handshake industry, but every owner is still findable online.

The exact channel mix per retailer — SEO vs local ads vs social — we won't guess at. We'll let the test phase tell us what actually moves the needle, then package the winner.

Phase 3 — scale

We productise the proven package and roll it across the retailer base: a standardised, co-op-fundable local-marketing offering that makes Sylvie indispensable to all 400, and a magnet for the next 400. Acquisition runs continuously alongside.
