## 2. The opportunity we see

Two things stood out when we looked under the hood:

- **You already have real authority — it's just aimed at the wrong targets.** Sylvie sits at
  a Domain Rating of 50 (Ahrefs) / Authority Score 41 (SEMrush). That's genuinely strong. But
  much of it is spent on informational traffic ("stones for July born" — 40,000 searches a
  month that don't sell a ring) and on head terms like "engagement rings" that the pure-B2C
  players own and a B2B brand structurally can't win. **We don't need to build authority from
  scratch. We need to redeploy the authority you already have** toward terms that map to your
  business.

- **Your retailer landing pages are your hidden asset.** You already have a page per retailer
  location. Some rank on page one already (Des Moines). They're simply under-optimised — which
  means the fastest wins on the board require **improving what exists, not building anything new.**

## 3. The plan — two engines

**Engine 1 — Retailer enablement (make Sylvie indispensable).**
Help each retailer sell locally: optimise their Sylvie landing page to rank for the local
"engagement rings [city]" searches, then extend to their own Google Business Profile and local
SEO, then drive walk-ins. In your words — *we become their agency as well as their supplier.*

**Engine 2 — Retailer acquisition (get from 400 to 800).**
Reach jeweler owners directly and sign new accounts — using your target-jeweler list to build
custom audiences on Meta and LinkedIn — plus consumer demand that makes jewelers *want* to
carry the line. (This is the "big LinkedIn play" we flagged; the jewelry world may be a
handshake industry, but every owner is still findable online.)

The exact channel mix per retailer — SEO vs local ads vs social — we **won't guess at.** We'll
let the test phase tell us what actually moves the needle, then package the winner.

## 4. How we start — a low-risk test phase

You were clear, and we agree completely: **nothing goes near a real retailer relationship until
we've proven ourselves.** A retailer relationship you've spent years building is not something
to experiment on.

So we prove the retailer model **on your own pages first** — same playbook, zero risk:

**We take your existing retailer landing pages and optimise them to rank.** The Portland /
Kassab Jewelers page is the perfect worked example. Here's what we found live on the call, and
what we'd fix:

| What we found | The fix |
|---|---|
| Page targets "engagement rings **in** Portland" (~20 searches/mo) | Retarget to "engagement rings Portland" (~400 searches/mo) — 20× the demand |
| The target keyword sits in an **H2**; the H1 is the retailer's name | Promote the keyword to the H1 |
| **No backlinks** point to the Portland page | Build direct links to it |
| Meta description too short | Rewrite for click-through |

This is the retailer enablement model in miniature — that page exists to win local searches and
send walk-ins to Kassab. Prove it here, on your asset, and we've proven it everywhere. In
parallel we handle foundational technical clean-up and begin the acquisition groundwork (turning
your jeweler list into ad-ready audiences).

**What the test phase proves:** that we execute, A-to-Z, hands-on — and that the model works —
before a single retailer is involved.
